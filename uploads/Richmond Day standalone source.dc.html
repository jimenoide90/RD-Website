We gave a sneak peek at an unreleased BMW to the only ones we could trust not to leak it.

The BMW M3 CS Handschalter (Manual) was different. Limited to just 40 units in Canada, it remained one of the brand’s best kept secrets, internally nicknamed the “super cool edition” to help keep the project under wraps.

To build anticipation for the launch, BMW decided to create a teaser. The challenge was finding someone who could see the vehicle without spilling the beans. BMW fans would leak it. Rival fans would leak it. Even internal stakeholders couldn’t necessarily be trusted.

So instead of showing the car to people most likely to obsess over it, we showed it to those least likely to embrace it: grandmas.

The contrast between the raw performance machine and the unimpressed reactions created a reveal that felt entirely M: confident and clever, without taking itself too seriously.
